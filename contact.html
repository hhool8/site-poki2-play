Contact

By Poki2 Team · Last updated 2026-10-03

If you have questions, partnership requests, or need support, please contact us:

  • Email: contact@poki2.online

Poki2 is a free browser gaming portal: we host and curate HTML5 games that run instantly online, with no download, no installation and no account required. Messages are read by the Poki2 team and answered within 2-3 business days. To help us reply faster, please include the page URL, the game title and, for technical problems, your browser and device model.

What you can contact us about

  • Game or page problems — a game that will not load, an error message, a broken link or a page that displays incorrectly. Include the URL and, if possible, a screenshot and the steps that reproduce the issue.
  • Removal and copyright requests — if you own the rights to a title listed on Poki2 and want it removed or credited differently, contact us via the DMCA page. Rights holders receive priority handling.
  • Game submissions and partnerships — developers and studios interested in publishing an HTML5 game on Poki2, or in a cross-promotion or licensing arrangement.
  • Advertising and sponsorships — for display, sponsorship or media kit requests.
  • Privacy and data requests — questions about our Privacy Policy, cookies, or data collected by third-party ad providers. See also our Terms of Service for the rules that apply to using the site.

Before you write

Many issues are solved by a quick refresh. If a game fails to start, reload the page, disable ad blockers for that page, and confirm that WebGL and JavaScript are enabled in your browser. Games on Poki2 run client-side in an iframe, so an extension that blocks scripts can prevent them from loading. If the problem persists, tell us the exact game and browser and we will investigate.

We do not provide phone support and we do not sell games, accounts or in-game currency. Anything claiming otherwise is not affiliated with Poki2.

For legal or copyright notices, see our DMCA page or Terms.
